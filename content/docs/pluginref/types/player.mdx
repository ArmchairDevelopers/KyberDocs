---

### name

> **string**

Player's username.

---

### playerId

> **integer**

Player's EA player id.

---

### team

> **integer**

Player's current team.

---

### battlepoints

> **integer**

Player's battlepoint count.

---

### score

> **integer**

The player's current score.

---

### kills

> **integer**

The player's current kill count.

---

### assists

> **integer**

The player's current assist count.

---

### deaths

> **integer**

The player's current death count.

---

### afkTime

> **number**

How long it has been since the player has inputted anything in seconds.

---

### characterEntity

> **[Entity](/g/pluginref/types/entity)**

The character entity the player is currently occupying, or nil if not spawned on foot.

---

### vehicleEntity

> **[Entity](/g/pluginref/types/entity)**

The vehicle entity the player is currently occupying, or nil if not in a vehicle.

---

### activeKit

> **[DataContainer](/g/pluginref/types/datacontainer)**

The active kit/loadout asset for the player, or nil if none is set.

---

### isBot

> **boolean**

If player is a bot.

---

### isAlive

> **boolean**

If player is actively spawned as a character or vehicle.

